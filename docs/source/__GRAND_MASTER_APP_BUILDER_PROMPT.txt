# GRAND MASTER APP BUILDER PROMPT

## PRODUCT NAME

**LeadGuard by abcstudio**

### Tagline

**No Lead Left Behind**

---

# 1. PRODUCT VISION

Build a modern client application called **LeadGuard by abcstudio**.

LeadGuard is the client control centre for businesses working with abcstudio.

It connects the four main abcstudio services:

1. Website Revamp
2. SEO + AEO
3. AI Chatbot
4. AI Voice Assistant

into one simple experience focused on helping the client:

**Get Found → Capture Enquiries → Respond → Follow Up → Convert → Grow**

LeadGuard must NOT become complicated enterprise CRM software.

It should be designed specifically for busy owners and small teams who want to know:

> **Who needs my attention today?**

That is the primary product question.

Every screen, feature and recommendation should support that question.

---

# 2. CORE PRODUCT PROMISE

LeadGuard helps a business owner:

- See incoming enquiries
- Know where each enquiry came from
- Know who has already been contacted
- Know who still needs a reply
- Track quotations
- Schedule follow-ups
- Identify leads becoming cold
- Rescue forgotten opportunities
- See website and visibility performance
- See chatbot-generated enquiries
- See voice-generated enquiries
- Request support from abcstudio
- Understand what should be improved next

The product philosophy is:

# NO LEAD LEFT BEHIND

---

# 3. TARGET USER

Primary users are:

- Owner-led service businesses
- Small businesses
- Local businesses
- Businesses without sophisticated sales systems
- Teams currently using email, messages, calls, spreadsheets or memory to manage enquiries

Assume users are:

- Busy
- Non-technical
- Frequently mobile
- More interested in customers and revenue than complicated analytics
- Unwilling to spend significant time learning software

Therefore:

**Simplicity is more important than feature quantity.**

---

# 4. PRODUCT EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

The interface should feel:

- Friendly
- Calm
- Modern
- Premium
- Clear
- Fast
- Human
- Encouraging

Avoid:

- Technical jargon

# 5. USER ROLES

Create four permission levels.

## A. Business Owner

Full access to their company's LeadGuard account.

Can:

- View all leads
- Manage leads
- View performance
- View Lead Rescue
- Add team members
- View recommendations
- Request abcstudio support
- Manage notification preferences

---

## B. Manager

Can:

- View leads
- Update leads
- Assign leads
- Set follow-ups
- View performance
- View Lead Rescue

Cannot:

- Manage billing
- Change account ownership
- Access sensitive account administration

---

## C. Staff

Can:

- View assigned leads
- Update assigned leads
- Add notes
- Record customer contact
- Set follow-up
- Mark actions complete

Performance information may be restricted.

---

## D. abcstudio Administrator

Separate administrative environment.

Can manage multiple client accounts.

The abcstudio admin must never be mixed visually with the client's normal interface.

# 19. LEAD RESCUE RULES

A lead may become a rescue candidate when:

- A new enquiry has not been answered
- Follow-up is overdue
- A quotation has not been followed up
- The customer previously expressed interest but communication stopped
- No activity has occurred for a configurable number of days
- A promised follow-up date was missed
- A call or conversation captured a lead but no next action exists

Do not automatically declare a lead "lost".

Instead state:

**This opportunity may need attention.**
